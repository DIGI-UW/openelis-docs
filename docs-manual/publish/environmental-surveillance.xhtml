Environmental Surveillance

At a glance

PurposeRegister, test, and report on environmental samples (water, soil, food, and similar) and judge each result against a regulatory compliance standard.
What makes it differentAn environmental order is tied to a sampling site rather than a patient, carries a per-sample manifest (one row per physical sample, with optional GPS coordinates and collection conditions), and each numeric result is evaluated PASS / FAIL against the limits of the linked compliance standard.
You’ll end up withValidated, regulation-scored results for each sample, ready for reporting.
Who does thisLaboratory staff for order entry, results, and validation. Access is set by user permissions.
Before you startThe compliance standards, environmental sample types, and (optionally) holding times must already be configured by an administrator — see Admin — Compliance Standards & Holding-Time Configuration.
WhereMain menu → Orders → Add Environmental Order, with the wizard steps Dashboard, Enter Order, Collect Sample, Label & Store, and QA Review. Results are entered under Results and reviewed under Validation.

The Dashboard step under Add Environmental Order is the landing view — a list of environmental orders in progress. The separate Compliance Dashboard (left menu) is a reporting view of testing trends and compliance KPIs, not the order-entry landing.

Create an environmental order

Step 1. Open Orders → Add Environmental Order → Enter Order. Enter or generate a Lab Number, confirm the Collection Date, and search the Sampling Site by name or code and choose Select on the matching site.

{{IMG:01-order-top.png}}

Figure 1. The top of the environmental order form (example data) — lab number, collection date, and the selected sampling site.

Step 2. Under Applicable Compliance Standards, tick the standard the order is judged against. In the Per-Sample Manifest, set the Sample Type and Container for each row (optionally GPS coordinates, location details, and the collected date/time). Open Tests & Panels and select at least one test.

{{IMG:01b-order-manifest.png}}

Figure 2. The compliance standard and per-sample manifest (example data).

Note. At least one test is required before the order will save. Selecting a compliance standard does not auto-select tests — you choose tests yourself. Choose Save & Next to advance to Label & Store.

Move through the order stages

Step 3. The order moves through the wizard stages shown at the top: Enter Order → Label & Store → QA Review (Collect Sample is available for workflows that record collection separately). On Label & Store, print the sample labels and either assign a storage location or tick skip storage, then continue. Save & Next unlocks once labels are printed and storage is handled. For the storage hierarchy and how to assign or locate samples, see Sample Storage Management.

{{IMG:05-label-store.png}}

Figure 3. The Label & Store stage — printing labels and assigning or skipping storage for each sample.

Step 4. On QA Review, work through the checklist (sampling site correct, sample types and tests correct, labels printed, storage assigned) and choose Submit to release the order into the results workflow.

{{IMG:02-qa-review.png}}

Figure 4. The QA Review step with its checklist, sampling site, and sample summary (example order).

Enter results and read the compliance evaluation

Step 5. Open Results → By Unit and select the test unit so the order's tests load into the grid (the screen is empty until a unit is selected). Enter each numeric result and choose Save.

{{IMG:03-results.png}}

Figure 5. Results entry by unit.

Step 6. After saving, reload the screen. The Status — Per Regulation column shows the verdict — PASS (green) or FAIL (red) with the standard name. The U (k = 2) column shows the expanded measurement uncertainty where configured. A result entered after a sample's SOP maximum holding time carries an internal warning in its Notes.

Note. The compliance evaluation is numeric-only and is computed on the server, so it appears after Save and a reload — not as you type.

Validate and release results

Step 7. Open Validation → Routine and select the test unit so its results and tests appear (empty until a unit is selected). Review each result, mark it accepted, and choose Save. The result is validated and released.

{{IMG:04-validation.png}}

Figure 6. Validation by unit (Routine).

Field reference

FieldNotes
Sampling SiteThe collection location; search by name or code, then choose Select.
Applicable Compliance StandardThe regulation results are judged against; selected manually.
Sample Type / ContainerSet per manifest row.
GPS Lat/Lng, Location Details, CollectedOptional per-sample details.
TestAt least one required before the order can save.
U (k = 2)Expanded measurement uncertainty, where configured.
Status — Per RegulationPASS/FAIL verdict; numeric-only; appears after save and reload.

Tips

  • Sampling-site search matches the start of the name or code — use a real prefix, then choose Select.
  • The compliance evaluation is numeric-only and shows after save and reload.
  • If electronic signatures are enabled, saving and validating results prompt for your signature.
  • Compliance standards and test holding times are maintained under Admin — Compliance Standards & Holding-Time Configuration.
