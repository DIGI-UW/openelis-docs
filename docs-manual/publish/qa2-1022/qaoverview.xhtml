Overview

QA Overview is the daily snapshot for the QA officer. It lists the items that need attention, today's indicator values, this week's counts, the status of each quality pillar and the most recent quality activity. Each line links to the screen where you act on it.

Quality Indicators has two screens. QI Dashboard shows each indicator for the reporting window with its target and action threshold, and links to a detail page. QI Configuration is where the targets and action thresholds are set, an indicator is switched on or off, and a test section is given its own thresholds.

The indicators are Average Turnaround Time, Rejection Rate, Amendment Rate, NCE Pulse and the optional Critical Callback Compliance. When an indicator's month-to-date value breaches its action threshold, OpenELIS creates a non-conforming event automatically, one per indicator per month (for example QI threshold breach, AMENDMENT (2026-10)).

Step 1: Read what needs attention today

Go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → QA Overview. The subtitle shows today's date.

ATTENTION REQUIRED lists, each with a count and an arrow to the matching screen: Critical NCEs pending acknowledgment, Overdue CAPAs, QC violations in last 24 hours, CAPA effectiveness reviews due and EQA submissions due in next 14 days. In the figure the critical NCE, overdue CAPA and effectiveness review rows are marked in red; the EQA row, which is not yet due, is grey.

Step 2: Check the week, the pillars and recent activity

Scroll down. THIS WEEK counts new NCEs (by severity), NCEs resolved, CAPAs completed, QC violations, open EQA submissions, audit log entries and signature events.

PILLAR STATUS gives one card each for Statistical QC, EQA, Quality Indicators and QMS & Improvement, marked with a tick when the pillar is on track and a cross when something is open (for example a critical NCE pending).

RECENT ACTIVITY lists the latest quality actions with their time and user. View full audit trail opens the audit trail.

Step 3: Answer the inspector's five questions

At the bottom of the page, open Inspector readiness. It answers five ISO 15189 quality questions, each with a tick, a cross or a dash and a link: Are runs in control?, How did EQA perform last cycle?, Are QIs on target?, Open critical NCEs? and Accreditation status?.

Figure 3. Inspector readiness expanded: no QC data yet, QIs on target (tick), one critical NCE pending (cross), and accreditation 1 of 1 in force (tick).

Step 4: Read the QI Dashboard

Go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → Quality Indicators → QI Dashboard. The Reporting window is Rolling 30 days; Last recomputed shows when the values were calculated, with a Refresh button beside it.

Each tile shows the value, the comparison with the prior 30 days, the target and action threshold (for example Target ≤ 24h · action ≥ 48h) and its basis. In the figure the coloured bar at the top of the tile is green for the two indicators within target and red for Amendment Rate, which is past its action threshold. Each tile also has an info icon.

{{IMG:04-qi-dashboard.png}}

Step 5: Open the detail behind an indicator

Press View detail on a tile. Amendment Rate opens its detail page, Results corrected after their patient report went out, with From and To dates, the rate, a trend chart with an Aggregation choice (Daily) and the target and action lines, a By test table and an Individual amendments table (time amended, lab number, test, prior and current value, who amended it, when it was released and the time to amend).

{{IMG:05-qi-amendment.png}}

Figure 5. Amendment Rate detail for 21 September to 21 October 2026: 6.68%, with a daily trend above the dashed action line from 19 October; By test begins with HDL cholesterol, 24 of 45 amended.

The other tiles open their own screens: Average TAT opens the Turn Around Time Report (period buttons, TAT Segment, Lab Unit, Test / Panel, Sample Type, Ordering Site, Calendar or Working Time, Priority, Include cancelled/rejected, then Generate Report with Summary, Detail List and Trends tabs). NCE Pulse opens All NCEs filtered to critical events pending.

Step 6: Set targets and action thresholds

Go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → Quality Indicators → QI Configuration. The table lists each indicator with Enabled, Target, Action threshold, the number of Overrides and an edit button under Actions. Critical Callback Compliance is off (No) until you switch it on.

{{IMG:09-qi-config.png}}

Figure 7. QI Configuration: Average Turnaround Time ≤ 24h target, ≥ 48h action threshold and one override; Critical Callback Compliance not enabled.

Step 7: Give a test section its own thresholds

Press the edit button on the indicator. The dialog has Default thresholds (Track this indicator, Target, Action threshold) and Per-section overrides. Press Add section override, choose the Test section and enter its target and action threshold. Sections without an override use the default. The trash icon removes an override. Press Save; the Overrides count in the table goes up by one.

{{IMG:10-qi-config-edit.png}}

Figure 8. Edit Average Turnaround Time: default 24 h target and 48 h action threshold, with a Molecular Biology override of 72 h and 96 h.

Tips

  • Because a breach of the action threshold creates a non-conforming event once per indicator per month, a high amendment rate early in the month shows up on All NCEs as QI threshold breach, AMENDMENT with the month.
  • Known issue OGC-1491: EQA samples are counted in turnaround time, the quality indicators and dashboard statistics as if they were patient samples.
  • On the test server the Individual amendments table lists corrections where the value did not change (for example 0.6 corrected to 0.60). These rows are counted in the amendment rate.
  • The turnaround time Target and Action threshold boxes in QI Configuration do not accept a value above 100 hours.
  • The EQA pillar card and the EQA question under Inspector readiness show Coming soon with the tag OGC-721, although the EQA screens are in use and EQA submissions are counted in ATTENTION REQUIRED and THIS WEEK.
  • Rejection Rate showed 0.00% (0 of 1384 started) on the test server for the whole window.
