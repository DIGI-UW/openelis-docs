Overview

Each non-conforming event (NCE) can carry any number of actions. A corrective action removes the cause of the event that happened; a preventive action stops a similar event from happening; a concurrent control action contains the problem while the cause is being fixed. Actions are entered on the Nonconforming Events Corrective Action form for the event and listed on the event's CAPA tab.

The CAPA Register collects every action from every event, with tiles for Open, Overdue, Due This Week and Completed (90d), so the QA officer can follow them without opening each event. The counts also feed QA Overview (Overdue CAPAs, CAPA effectiveness reviews due).

Step 1: Open the event's CAPA tab

Go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → QMS & Improvement → Non-Conform → All NCEs and press the event. The event opens in place with the tabs Event Details, Investigation, CAPA (with the number of actions) and History. The CAPA tab lists the actions under CORRECTIVE ACTIONS, each with the person responsible, due date and completed date. Add opens the corrective action form for this event.

{{IMG:01-nce-capa-tab.png}}

Figure 1. NCE-2026-00001, a haemolysed EDTA sample from an outpatient clinic, at status CAPA: one corrective action, refresher training for outpatient phlebotomists, with Ruth Kopi responsible, due 2026-11-05 and completed 2026-10-20.

Step 2: Find the event on the corrective action form

Press Add on the CAPA tab, or go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → QMS & Improvement → Non-Conform → Corrective actions, set Search By to NCE Number (or Lab Number), enter the value and press Search. The form shows the event: NCE Number, NCE Date, Severity, Reporting Unit, Specimen(s), Lab Number, Reporting Date, Description of Event, NCE Category and NCE Type.

{{IMG:02-ca-form-top.png}}

Figure 2. The corrective action form for NCE-2026-00001: reported 09/10/2026 by Hematology on a serum specimen, category Sample, type Hemolytic sample.

Step 3: Record the action and submit

Scroll to the actions table. The empty row at the top is for the new action; actions already recorded are shown below it and cannot be changed here. Fill in:

  • Corrective actions: what will be done.
  • Person responsible: a name and role, typed as free text.
  • Date Completed and Due Date (dd/mm/yyyy).
  • Action Type: Corrective actions, preventive action or concurrent control action (at least one).

Turnaround Time is filled in for you: the number of days from the NCE date to the date completed. You can also record a Date of discussion with NCE Staff above the table, and, under NCE Resolution, whether the actions were effective (Yes or No) with the date. Press Submit. The form returns to the search, and the action appears on the event's CAPA tab and in the CAPA Register.

{{IMG:03-ca-form-filled.png}}

Figure 3. A preventive action for NCE-2026-00001: add a haemolysis check to the outpatient sample reception checklist, Ruth Kopi, phlebotomy supervisor, completed 22/10/2026, due 15/11/2026, turnaround 13 days. The earlier corrective action is shown greyed below.

Step 4: Follow all actions in the CAPA Register

Go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → QMS & Improvement → CAPA Register. The tiles count Open, Overdue, Due This Week and Completed (90d). The table lists every action with NCE Number, Corrective / Preventive Action, Type, Person Responsible, Due Date, Completed and Status.

{{IMG:04-capa-register.png}}

Figure 4. CAPA Register with four actions on three events: three Open and one Overdue (the HDL cholesterol calibration review on NCE-2026-00004, due 2026-10-16).

Step 5: Filter the register

Use Status (All, Open, Overdue or Completed), Assignee and Due Date (From) and (To) to narrow the list. The table updates as you change a filter. When nothing matches, the register shows No CAPAs match these filters.

{{IMG:05-capa-overdue.png}}

Tips

  • The form will not submit without a Date Completed. The message asks you to fill in the action, person responsible, date completed and at least one action type, or to leave the row empty. A planned action that is not yet done therefore cannot be recorded through the form on this version.
  • On the test server the register status does not take the Completed date into account: actions with a Completed date show Open, or Overdue when the due date has passed (NCE-2026-00004, completed 2026-10-22 and due 2026-10-16, shows Overdue), and Completed (90d) stays at 0.
  • After Submit no confirmation message appears; check the event's CAPA tab or the register to see the new action.
  • On the corrective action form, an action recorded earlier shows its Due Date as a long number (for example 1793836800000) instead of a date.
  • On the CAPA tab, Person Responsible, Due Date and Completed run together on one line without spaces.
  • Severity is empty on the corrective action form for an event that is Critical on All NCEs.
  • The Type column shows the type as stored (for example preventive action in lower case and Corrective actions in the plural).
