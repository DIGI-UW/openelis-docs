Overview

An in-house scheme is an EQA scheme that your laboratory runs itself, so it needs no provider. For each cycle, the blinding wizard records the samples, their target values and acceptance ranges, how the material was prepared, and which analyst runs which sample. Sealing the panel encrypts the target values and creates one blinded order per sample. The analysts see only a blind code such as IH-9-01. Revealing a target before the unblind date takes the unblind privilege and is recorded in the audit trail.

Schemes run by an outside provider are covered on EQA participant: take part in a proficiency testing scheme and EQA provider: run a proficiency testing scheme.

Step 1: Add the in-house scheme

Go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → EQA → EQA Management → Schemes and press Add Scheme. In Add New EQA Scheme:

  • Scheme Name: for example the section and the purpose.
  • Scheme type: In-house. The Provider field disappears, because in-house schemes need no provider.
  • Description: what the scheme checks and how often.
  • Assigned Tests: the tests the scheme covers. The wizard offers only these tests.
  • Record the analyst on every result: switch on to record who ran each sample.

Press Add Scheme. The scheme is added to the list with Scheme type In-house.

{{IMG:01-scheme-add-inhouse.png}}

Figure 1. Add New EQA Scheme with Scheme type In-house: no Provider field. The example shown is a haematology scheme being filled in; the scheme used for the rest of this page is "Biochemistry in-house blind panel" with Glucose (Plasma) and HDL cholesterol (Serum) assigned and the analyst recorded.

Step 2: Start a cycle

Go to Main menu → Quality Assurance → EQA → In-House Blinding, choose the scheme under In-house scheme and press Launch blinding wizard. The wizard Create in-house blinded panel has four steps: Scheme & cycle, Samples & prep, Analysts and Confirm & seal.

In the first step enter the Cycle name, the Planned start date and the Unblind date (submission deadline), then press Next. The analysts must submit their results by the unblind date; the targets are revealed after it.

{{IMG:02-wizard-scheme-cycle.png}}

Figure 2. Scheme & cycle: Biochemistry in-house blind panel, cycle "Q1 2027 blind re-test", planned start 11 January 2027 and unblind date 29 January 2027 (shown in the browser's date format).

Step 3: Define the samples and how they were prepared

For each sample (S01, S02, and more with Add sample) choose the Test and enter the Target value, Unit, Range low and Range high. Every sample needs a test and a target value.

Under Prep & aliquoting details record the Source (Patient-sample pool, aliquoted in-house; Vendor-supplied material; or Mixed sources), Lot number, Aliquots produced (at least one per sample), Storage temperature, Expiration date and Homogeneity QC passed with notes. If homogeneity QC has not passed, a written justification is needed to distribute. Press Next.

{{IMG:03-wizard-samples.png}}

Figure 3. Two samples: S01 Glucose (Plasma), target 0.95 g/l (0.85 to 1.05), and S02 HDL cholesterol (Serum), target 0.45 g/l (0.40 to 0.50); patient-sample pool, lot RS-2701-A, 4 aliquots, refrigerated, expiring 31 March 2027, homogeneity QC passed.

Step 4: Choose the analysts

Under Scheme analysts select the OpenELIS users who will run the samples; they join the scheme's roster. Choose the Assignment mode:

  • Round-robin: one analyst per sample, in rotation.
  • Identical set: every analyst tests the same samples.
  • Manual per-sample assignment: you choose the analyst for each sample.

The table shows the analyst for each sample. The assignment is recorded on the blinded order and follows it through result entry. Press Next.

{{IMG:04-wizard-analysts.png}}

Figure 4. Two analysts selected with Round-robin: S01 Glucose to Open ELIS and S02 HDL cholesterol to Grace Tau.

Step 5: Confirm and seal

The last step summarises the scheme, cycle, number of samples and blinded aliquots, aliquots produced, lot number, unblind date and homogeneity QC. A warning explains that sealing encrypts the target values and that the panel cannot be edited afterwards. Label sheets carry the blind code, cycle and analyte, never a target value. Press Seal panel & distribute.

{{IMG:05-wizard-confirm.png}}

Figure 5. Confirm & seal for the Q1 2027 cycle: 2 samples, 2 blinded aliquots, 4 aliquots produced, lot RS-2701-A, unblind date 2027-01-29.

Step 6: Note the blind codes

Panel sealed and distributed lists the blind codes that the blinded orders carry as their sample IDs, the unblind date and the assigned analysts. From here you can Print label sheet, Open My Cycles, Open the Workplan or go Back to in-house panels.

{{IMG:06-wizard-sealed.png}}

Figure 6. Panel sealed and distributed: blind codes IH-9-01 and IH-9-02, unblind date 29/01/2027, assigned analysts Open ELIS and Grace Tau.

Step 7: Follow the panels

Back on In-House Blinding, with the scheme chosen, the tiles count panels Sealed, awaiting distribution, In testing, Unblinding within 7 days and Scored or closed. Each panel row shows the cycle, number of samples, Target values (Sealed), Status, the unblind date and the actions Print label sheet and Unblind now.

{{IMG:07-inhouse-list.png}}

Figure 7. In-House Blinding for the biochemistry scheme: the Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 panels, both Sealed and DISTRIBUTED. The In testing tile counts three because a duplicate Q1 2027 panel created during capture is below the area shown.

Step 8: Run the blinded orders

The analysts find the blinded orders in result entry and the Workplan by their blind code, like any other order. In Main menu → Results → Results Entry, search for the blind code and press Load results. The row shows the test and sample type with the test's usual reference range; the target value is not shown.

{{IMG:08-results-blind-code.png}}

Figure 8. Results Entry for IH-9-01: Glucose (Plasma), Not started, with the reference range 0.70 to 1.10 g/l.

Tips

  • Seal only once. Known issue OGC-1487: retrying after a failed seal creates a second panel, and every seal creates a blank patient record.
  • Check the range values before sealing. Known issue OGC-1486: the range fields accept letters until the seal step, and the in-house wizard does not check that Range low is below Range high.
  • Choose an unblind date in the future. Known issue OGC-1484: the date is not checked, and a past unblind date is unblinded automatically.
  • Known issue OGC-1447: the blinding wizard can briefly show that the scheme has no tests before the list loads.
  • Known issue OGC-1491: EQA samples, including blinded orders, are counted in turnaround time and the quality indicators as if they were patient samples.
  • Enter the Unit to match the test's reporting unit (here g/l), so the target and the reported result can be compared.
  • The EQA material panel column on In-House Blinding repeats the cycle name.
  • The breadcrumb reads Home / In-House Blinding, without Quality Assurance or EQA.
