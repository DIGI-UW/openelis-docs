The OpenELIS Global manual for laboratory staff and for the administrators who set OpenELIS up. Each page says which OpenELIS version it was checked against. Pages marked Older format come from the previous manual and are being rewritten; they are still the best guide for those workflows. Pages marked In review are published before their review is finished, so you can use them now; details may still change.
