How-to · Reception staff

At a glance

Purpose

Register a new patient so they can be linked to test orders.

You'll end up with

A saved patient record, found by patient search and in order entry.

Who does this

Reception staff. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.

Before you start

The patient's name, sex and date of birth, and their identifiers if they have them. Search first (see Patient Management) so you do not register someone twice.

Where

Main menu → Patient → Add/Edit Patient → New Patient

The New Patient form captures a patient's core identity plus optional contact, emergency and identification details. Identifier labels can be set by your site; the figures show the labels used on the test server, where the patient identifier is called Patient ID / Clinic ID / UR number.

Step 1: Open the New Patient form

  1. Open Main menu → Patient → Add/Edit Patient. The Add Or Modify Patient page opens on Search for Patient.

Add/Edit patient

  1. Click New Patient. The Patient Information form opens.

New Patient

Step 2: Enter the patient's details

  1. Enter the patient identifier (Patient ID / Clinic ID / UR number on the test server) and the National ID if you have them. Whether National ID is required depends on your site's patient configuration; on the test server it was optional.

  2. Enter Last Name and First Name, and optionally Primary phone (the hint under it shows the local format, here Local: xxxx-xxxx) and Email.

  3. Choose Sex (required). Enter Date of Birth (required) as dd/mm/yyyy or pick it from the calendar. Age/Years, Months and Days fill in from the date of birth. Sex and Date of Birth are required while the Patient sex required and Patient age required settings in Order Entry Configuration are true, as they were on the test server (see the General Configurations reference).

A new patient with identifier, name, phone, sex and date of birth. The age fields have filled in.

panel_c1

  1. Optionally click Add Photo, and expand Emergency Contact Info, Additional Information and Identification Documents to record more.

Step 3: Save

  1. Click Save at the bottom of the form (Clear empties it). If Sex or Date of Birth is missing, nothing is saved and the fields are marked: Sex is Required and Patient Birth date Required.

    Save with only a name: Sex and Date of Birth are marked as required.

panel_c1

When the record is saved, the form becomes read-only and an Edit switch appears next to Patient Information. Use it to correct anything straight away.

The saved patient, read-only, with the Edit switch.

Field reference

Field

Notes

Patient identifier (Patient ID / Clinic ID / UR number on the test server)

Optional on the test server. Searchable as Patient Id.

National ID

Optional on the test server. Your site's patient configuration can make it required.

Last Name, First Name

The patient's name.

Primary phone, Email

Optional. The phone hint shows the local format.

Sex

Required when Patient sex required is true (the 3.3.0 test server value). Male or Female.

Date of Birth

Required when Patient age required is true (the 3.3.0 test server value). dd/mm/yyyy. Age/Years, Months and Days are calculated from it.

Emergency Contact Info, Additional Information, Identification Documents

Optional sections that expand for further detail.

Notes

  • A patient can also be registered from order entry. See Enter a clinical order, which also checks for possible matches before creating a patient.

  • Duplicate records are combined with Patient → Merge Patient.

  • Known issue (3.3.0-SNAPSHOT): the Sex is Required message shows twice under the Sex field.
