How-to · Reception staff

At a glance

Purpose

Combine two duplicate patient records into a single record.

You'll end up with

One merged patient record; the duplicate's data folded into the primary.

Who does this

Reception staff. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.

Before you start

The two patient records you know to be duplicates, and which one should be kept as the primary.

Where

Main menu → Patient → Merge Patient.

Merge Patient Records is a three-step wizard (Select Patients, Select Primary, then Confirm Merge) that joins two duplicate records into one.

Step 1: Select the two patients

  1. Open Main menu. Then, click Patient

Click patient

  1. Click Merge Patient

Merge Patient

  1. Under Select First Patient, search by Patient Id, Last Name, First Name, Date of Birth, or Gender, and select the match.

Search Patient

  1. Select the patient.

Select patient

  1. Under Select Second Patient, search by Patient Id, Last Name, First Name, Date of Birth, or Gender, and select the match.

Search second patient

  1. Select the second patient.

Select second patient

  1. Click Next Step.

Step 2: Confirm the merge

  1. Select which patient should become the primary record. Then click Next

Select primary record patient

  1. On Confirm Merge, review the details and confirm. The records are combined into the primary.

Confirm Merge

Notes

  • Merging combines the records permanently, so verify the two are genuinely the same patient before confirming.

  • Use Cancel at any step to abandon the merge.
