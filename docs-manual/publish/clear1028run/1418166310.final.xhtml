At a glance

Purpose

Register a new patient so they can be linked to test orders.

You’ll end up with

A saved patient record, searchable and available when creating orders.

Who does this

Reception / registration staff. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.

Before you start

The patient’s identifying details — National ID, name, sex, and date of birth; a photo, if your site captures one.

Where

Main menu → Patient → Add / Edit Patient.

Step-by-step

Step 1. Open the Add or Modify Patient screen from the Patient menu.

Add edit patient

Step 2. The search panel opens first.

The Add or Modify Patient screen opens on the search panel.

Step 2. Search before you add. Enter any known detail — Patient ID, last/first name, or date of birth — and choose Search for Patient. Review the results to confirm the patient is not already registered. (Use External Search to look the patient up in a connected national registry.)

Step 3. If no match is found, choose New Patient. The full Patient Information form appears.

The blank Patient Information form. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk.

Step 4. Enter the patient details. At minimum complete the required fields (National ID, Last Name, First Name, Gender, and Date of Birth). Age is calculated automatically from the date of birth. Optional sections — Emergency Contact Info, Additional Information, and Identification Documents — can be expanded as needed.

A completed patient record (example data shown). Age fills in automatically once a date of birth is entered.

Step 5. Choose Save to create the patient record. The patient is now available when creating orders. Choose Clear to discard the entries and start over.

Select Save to commit the new patient record.

Tips

  • Searching first prevents duplicate patient records, which are difficult to merge later.

  • If the date of birth is unknown, you can enter an estimated age directly in the Age fields.

  • Use Add Photo to attach a patient photograph where local policy permits.
