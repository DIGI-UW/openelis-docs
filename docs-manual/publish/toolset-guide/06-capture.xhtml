Goal of this page. Get screenshots or a short walkthrough video of the live software on their own: for the website, release notes, a training deck or a design review. (For manual pages, Part 5 captures automatically.)

Capture is done by the openelis-qa plugin's evidence skill, which both the QA work and the manual use. You just describe what you want to see.

Ask for it

text text

What you get

  • Screenshots at a readable size (1440 by 900, sharp on high-resolution screens), one per step, named in order. Long pages are cropped to the part that matters.
  • A walkthrough video when you ask for one, slowed so a person can follow.
  • Personal data masked: patient names and identifiers are hidden. Test data is made to look realistic (no "TEST" names).

Claude shows you the pictures in the chat. Tell it where they should go (the manual project, the website repository, a Confluence page) and it puts them there.

Which site

  • testing.openelis-global.org: the default, with the current development version.
  • pngtest.openelis-global.org: quality screens (EQA, QC) and environmental and vector samples. Claude may add data there to make a screen look real.

Say which one if your feature only exists on one of them.

Good to know

  • A full capture can take a few minutes; Claude runs it in the background.
  • Screenshots are checked by eye after capture: a capture that "succeeded" can still show the wrong screen, and Claude looks before using it.
  • French screenshots (the French interface) exist only for the most used pages; other French pages keep the English pictures.
