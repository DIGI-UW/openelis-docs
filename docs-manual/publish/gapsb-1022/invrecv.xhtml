At a glance

PurposeRecord a delivery of reagents and consumables lot by lot, and report on what was received, consumed, held or about to expire.
You'll end up withNew lots on the Items board with their lot numbers, expiry dates and quantities, and a report you can read on screen or export.
Who does thisStore keepers and laboratory managers. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.
Before you startEach item in the delivery must already be defined on the Items board (see Manage inventory items, lots, counts and reorders).
WhereMain menu → Inventory → Receive, and Main menu → Inventory → Reports

Part A: Receive a delivery

The Receive page takes a whole delivery at once. As its introduction says: scan a box, type how much arrived, add it, scan the next. Nothing is saved until you confirm the whole delivery.

Step 1: Add each line of the delivery

Go to Main menu → Inventory → Receive. For each box or batch:

  1. Scan the box label into Scan the box. A GS1 label fills in the product, batch and expiry; anything it does not carry can be typed below.
  2. Check or choose the Item Name, and fill in the Lot Number, Expiration Date and Current Quantity (the quantity that arrived, in the item's units).
  3. Press Add to delivery. The line is added to the table below, and the fields clear for the next box.

Each line in the table has Remove in case it was added by mistake.

Figure 1. A delivery with two lines: Determine HIV-1/2 rapid test lot 64913K188, 500 tests, and EDTA blood collection tube 4 mL lot 6198844, 600 tubes, ready to receive.

Step 2: Confirm the delivery

When every line is in the table, press the receive button, which counts the lines (for example Receive 2 lines). To abandon the whole delivery, press Discard delivery. Receiving saves every line at once. A message confirms it, for example Success 2 lines received., and the form clears for the next delivery. The new lots appear under their items on the Items board.

Part B: Run an inventory report

Step 1: Choose the report and its options

Go to Main menu → Inventory → Reports and choose the Report Type:

  • Received: what arrived in the period, by item and lot.
  • Consumed: what was used in the period, totalled by item.
  • Stock on hand: what you hold, lot by lot, with expiry dates. As of sets the date; leave it blank for stock as it stands now.
  • Expiring: lots whose expiry falls in a window you choose (Expiring from and Expiring to), with an extra option, Include expired lots.

Received and Consumed need a Date Range (Start Date and End Date). Under Tags, pick tags to limit the report to items carrying any of them. Tick Include inactive items if needed. Press Run report.

Figure 3. Reports with Stock on hand chosen, As of left blank, and the tag Central laboratory selected.

Step 2: Read or export the report

The report appears under the options. Stock on hand lists each lot with Item Code, Item Name, Tags, Lot Number, Expiration Date, Quantity, Location, Status and QC Status, and ends with a total row. To save it, choose the Export Format and press Export.

Figure 4. Stock on hand for the Central laboratory tag: five lots, all Active and QC Passed, none assigned to a storage location, 1743 units in total.

Field reference

Field or controlWhat it shows or does
Scan the boxReads a GS1 barcode and fills the item, batch and expiry it carries.
Item Name, Lot Number, Expiration Date, Current QuantityOne line of the delivery. Current Quantity is the amount that arrived, in the item's units.
Add to delivery, RemoveAdds the line to the delivery table, or takes it out again.
Receive N lines, Discard deliverySaves the whole delivery, or abandons it. Nothing is saved before Receive.
Report TypeReceived, Consumed, Stock on hand or Expiring.
Date Range, As ofThe period for Received and Consumed, or the expiry window for Expiring; the date for Stock on hand (blank means now).
TagsLimits the report to items carrying any of the chosen tags.
Include inactive items, Include expired lotsAdds deactivated items to the report; Include expired lots appears for the Expiring report.
Export Format, ExportSaves the report as a file (CSV on the test server).

Tips

  • Use the same tags on items as on the board; a report filtered by tag then matches what each team sees on its board.
  • New lots need their QC status set before they can be drawn for use. Set it from the lot's menu on the Items board (Update QC Status).
  • Low-stock items also raise Low stock alerts on the Alerts dashboard (Main menu → Alerts); see Step 16 of Manage inventory items, lots, counts and reorders.
  • To add one lot of a single item, you can also use Receive stock on the item's row on the Items board.
  • The Report Tips panel suggests exporting to Excel; the export format offered on the test server was CSV.
