At a glance

PurposeFind patient reports whose results have been released, print them, and reprint or reissue them when results are added or corrected.
You'll end up withA printed patient report (PDF) with a version number, recorded in the order's version history so you can see what was issued, when, by whom and what changed.
Who does thisReception or reporting staff who hand out or dispatch results. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.
Before you startAt least one result on the order must be validated and released. Know the lab number, the patient, or the facility you are printing for.
WhereMain menu → Reports → Routine → Report Print Queue

Overview

The Report Print Queue lists patient reports as soon as their first result is released. Each row shows whether the report is complete (Partial, Final or Amended) and whether its current content has been printed (Unprinted or Printed). Printing from the queue issues a numbered version of the redesigned patient report. A printed report comes back to the queue when more results are released or a printed result is corrected, so nothing released goes out of date without being noticed.

Step 1: Open the queue

Go to Main menu → Reports → Routine → Report Print Queue. The list loads on open; you do not need to press Search.

At the top of the page:

  • An information line: Reports land here when their first result is released. Printed reports come back when more results are released or a printed result changes.
  • A legend for the five tags used in the list: Partial (some results are not released yet), Final (every result is released), Amended (a printed result changed, or the report was reissued with a correction), Unprinted (the current content has not been printed yet) and Printed (the last print matches what is released).
  • Two counters, To print and Amended to print.
  • The lookup and filter controls: Lab Number, Range, Search by patient, State, Print status, Time window, Facility, Ward / Dept / Unit and Requester.

The list has the columns Lab No / Name, Subject, Details, Released / Submitted, State, Print status, Version and Action. The newest release is at the top. An unprinted report shows Not issued in the Version column.

Figure 1. The Report Print Queue on open, with the state legend, the To print and Amended to print counters, the filters, and the newest released report at the top of the list.

Step 2: Narrow the list to what you are printing

Use the filters to show only the reports you need. For example, to print the reports for one clinic, type at least two characters in Facility and pick the clinic from the list. The chosen facility appears as a tag under the filters, the Ward / Dept / Unit filter becomes available, Clear filters appears, and the counters recount for the filtered list.

The other filters work the same way:

  • State: Amended, Final or Partial.
  • Print status: All, Unprinted or Printed.
  • Time window: Last 24 hours, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, All time or Custom range. As the help text says, the window applies to printed reports; unprinted reports always show.
  • Requester: type at least two characters of the requesting provider's name.

Figure 2. The queue filtered to Kila Kila Urban Clinic: the facility tag, Clear filters, and the counters showing 2 to print and 0 amended to print. The top row is a Partial 1/3 report that has not been issued.

Step 3: Check what a report contains

Press the arrow at the start of a row to expand it. The expanded row shows:

  • Tests: each test with its Result, Flag, the time it was Released, and who it was Validated by.
  • Order: Facility, Ward / Dept / Unit, Requester, Collected and Received.
  • Version history: one line per issued version, with Version, Issued, By, Change, Completeness (released results out of total) and Fingerprint, and an Open PDF link to the archived copy of that version.

In Figure 3 the report was first issued as v1 (First issue, 3/3). HDL cholesterol was then corrected and re-released, which made the row Amended. Printing it again issued v2, tagged Amended, with the change HDL cholesterol 0.50 → 0.58 g/l (Result corrected).

Figure 3. An expanded Amended row: the three tests with their release times and validator, the order details, and a version history with v1 (First issue) and v2 (Amended, HDL cholesterol corrected).

Step 4: Print the report

Press Print on the row. OpenELIS produces the report as a PDF and records a new version if the content has changed since the last print. A green message confirms the print, for example 1 report ready. Printed version 1 of DEV01260000000001248, and the row changes to Printed with the version number (here v1).

When this step was driven, pressing Print opened a new browser tab for the PDF. In the capture browser the PDF was saved as a download with a generated file name rather than displayed; how it opens depends on your browser's PDF settings.

Figure 4. After Print: the confirmation "1 report ready, Printed version 1 of DEV01260000000001248" and the row now Final, Printed, v1.

Step 5: Read the printed report

The patient report printed from the queue has these parts, top to bottom:

  • The report title, for example Results: final report.
  • A PATIENT block (name, age and sex, Date of Birth, Patient ID, National ID, URAP Number), an ORDER block (Lab Number, Program, Requester, Site / ward) and a DATES block (Order Date, Completed).
  • A SAMPLES table with Collected, Received and Condition.
  • The results table (Test, SMP, Method, Result, Flag, Reference range, Units, Completed), grouped by section such as BIOCHEMISTRY. Results outside the reference range are printed in bold with a flag such as ↑ H and a bar in the margin.
  • GENERAL COMMENTS, VALIDATED AND RELEASED BY with the validator and time, and End of report.
  • A legend of the flags, and a footer with the version and issue time (for example Version 1 · Issued 22/10/26 00:39), the lab number and the page number.

Figure 5. The top of a version 1 final report with two results flagged above normal, and the page footer showing the version, issue time, lab number and page.

A corrected report says so in a box above the patient details, naming the version it replaces and the change, for example Version 2. Corrected report. Replaces the report issued 22/10/26 00:26. Change: HDL cholesterol 0.50 → 0.58 g/l (Result corrected). The corrected test carries a note with the time of the correction.

Figure 6. A version 2 corrected report: the correction box, the HDL cholesterol result with its "Result corrected" note, and the footer reading Version 2.

Step 6: Look up a specific order

To go straight to one order, scan or type its lab number in Lab Number and press Enter. The lookup shows as a tag (for example Lab No: DEV01260000000001254) with Clear search next to it and the note Other filters are off while you look up a specific order; the other filters are greyed out while the lookup is active. Press Clear search to return to the filtered list.

Figure 7. A lab number lookup: the Lab No tag, Clear search, the greyed-out filters and the single matching row.

To look up a run of lab numbers, turn Range on, enter the first lab number and the To lab number, and press Enter. The tag then reads Lab No: [first] to [last].

Figure 8. A range lookup from DEV01260000000001245 to DEV01260000000001249, with Range turned On and the matching reports listed.

Step 7: Find all reports for a patient

Press Search by patient. A patient search opens with Patient Id, Previous Lab Number, Last Name, First Name, Date of Birth and Sex, and the buttons Search, External Search and Clear. Enter what you know, press Search, and select the patient in the results.

Figure 9. Search by patient: the search fields with Last Name filled in, and one matching patient in the results.

The queue then lists that patient's released reports, with the tag Patient: [name], Clear search, and the other filters off. Reports from every facility are listed, not only the facility you had filtered on before.

Figure 10. The queue after selecting the patient: two released reports for Raka, Michael from two facilities.

Tips

  • Work from To print and Amended to print: filter by facility, then use Print status = Unprinted to see only what still needs to go out.
  • An Amended row means a result that was already printed has changed. Print it again so the clinician gets the corrected report; the new version names the change.
  • A Partial report can be printed. When the remaining results are released the row comes back as Final and Unprinted.
  • Lookups by lab number, range or patient ignore the facility and other filters, so they can show orders from other facilities.
  • The times in the queue are shown in your browser's time zone, while the times printed on the report are the server's. On the test server these differed (for example 5:26 PM in the queue and 00:26 on the report). Check your server's time zone setting if they differ at your site. Reported as OGC-1550.
  • In the expanded row, Collected showed a dash for every order checked, although the printed report shows a collection date and time for the same orders. This is a known issue on the instance documented here (OGC-1550).
  • For a patient with no national ID, the Subject column shows a number in place of the national ID (OGC-1550).
  • The laboratory name and logo above the report title come from configuration: SiteName and headerLeftImage on Printed Report Configuration. Until they are set, reports print without them. See Admin: Set the patient report header (Printed Report Configuration).
  • Patient ID and URAP Number print blank when the patient record does not hold them; the test patients had only a National ID. Program printed blank on an order that had a program set. Adding a Patient ID after a report was printed did not change the reprint of that version.
  • Printing a report that is already Printed reprints the same version: the queue showed Reprinted version 1 and the PDF kept its original Issued time.

Not yet available

  • Printing several selected reports at once as one combined PDF (planned under OGC-1031).
  • Report types other than the patient report, and a progress status while a large report is generated (planned as the second phase of OGC-1031).
  • A reissue action without a result change. The legend mentions reports reissued with a correction, but no separate reissue control is shown on the rows.
  • Emailing a report from the queue. Messages the laboratory sends are listed on Sent Messages.
