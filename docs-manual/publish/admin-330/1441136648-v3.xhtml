How-to · Reception staff

At a glance

Purpose

Find an existing patient to view or edit their record, or start registering a new one.

You'll end up with

The right patient record open for review or editing.

Who does this

Reception staff. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.

Before you start

An identifier or name for the patient you are looking for.

Where

Main menu → Patient → Add/Edit Patient.

The Add Or Modify Patient screen is where you look up patients. Search by identifier or name, pick from the results to view or edit, or start a brand-new patient record.

Step 1 — Search for a patient

  1. Open Main menu. Then, click on Patient.

Click patient

  1. Click on Add/Edit Patient.

Add patient

  1. On the Search for Patient tab, enter any of Patient Id, Previous Lab Number, Last Name, First Name, Date of Birth, or Gender.

Add Or Modify Patient: the search fields and the Patient Results list.

  1. Click Search (or External Search to query a configured external source).

Search patient

Step 2 — Open, edit, or add

  1. In Patient Results, select the matching patient (columns include Last Name, First Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Unique Health ID number, National ID, Data Source Name) to view or edit the record.

Select patient

  1. To register someone new, click New Patient and complete the patient form (see the Adding a Patient guide).

New patient
