The Add Or Modify Patient page is where you look up patients. Search by identifier or name, select a result to view or edit the record, or start a new patient record. Identifier labels can be set by your site; the figures show the test server's labels.

Step 1: Search for a patient

  1. Open Main menu → Patient → Add/Edit Patient. The page opens on Search for Patient.

  2. Enter any of Patient Id, Previous Lab Number, Last Name, First Name, Date of Birth or Sex, and click Search. Clear empties the fields. External Search queries an external patient source when one is configured; it was unavailable on the test server.

  3. The results list shows Last Name, First Name, Sex, Date of Birth, the patient identifier, National ID and Data Source Name (OpenElis for records held in OpenELIS).

    {{IMG:01-search-results.png}}

    Figure 1. Searching by last name finds one patient.

Step 2: Open and edit a record

  1. Select the patient with the button at the start of their row. Their record opens under Patient Information, read-only.

    {{IMG:02-record-view.png}}

    Figure 2. The selected patient's record, read-only, with the Edit switch.

  2. Turn on the Edit switch next to Patient Information. It reads Editing and the fields become editable. Make your changes and click Save at the bottom of the form.

    {{IMG:03-record-editing.png}}

    Figure 3. The record with Editing on.

Step 3: Register someone new

If the patient is not found, click New Patient and complete the form. See Register a New Patient.
