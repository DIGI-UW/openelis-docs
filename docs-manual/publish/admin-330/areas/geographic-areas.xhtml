OpenELIS 3.3.0. This page describes the Locations & Organizations admin introduced in 3.3.0 (not yet released at the time of writing). It is a draft for review; see also Admin: Manage organizations (3.3.0).

At a glance

PurposeKeep the list of your country's administrative areas (for example Region, Province, District and LLG) that patient addresses, organizations and sampling sites point to.
You'll end up withAreas arranged in a tree under their parents, active and available in the Location pickers.
Who does thisAdministrators. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.
Before you startGeographic levels must exist before any area can be added. Most deployments load levels and areas once from the national list with Import / Export, then add or correct areas here.
WhereMain menu → Admin → Locations & Organizations → Geographic Areas

Geographic areas are the administrative divisions of the country. Each area has a name, a code and a level, and sits inside an area of the level above. They are used for patient addresses and to say where each organization and sampling site is. Pickers that filter by location include the areas beneath the one you choose.

Step 1: Browse the areas

  1. Open Main menu → Admin → Locations & Organizations → Geographic Areas. Until geographic levels exist, the list is empty and Add is unavailable.

  2. The list shows top-level areas first. Its columns are Name, Code, Level, Child areas (how many areas sit directly inside), Active and Actions. Expand a row with its arrow to load its children, or use Expand all and Collapse all.

  3. Use Search areas by name or code to find an area, and Status (Active, Inactive or All) to include inactive areas.

    {{IMG:01-tree.png}}

    Figure 1. Areas for three regions of Papua New Guinea, expanded. Each row's Actions offer the level below (Province, District, LLG) and Edit.

Step 2: Add an area

  1. To add an area inside another one, click the next level's button on the parent row, for example District + on Morobe Province. A row opens under the parent, labelled with the level and the parent (New District · Morobe Province).

  2. Enter the name (required) and an optional Code, then click Save. Save stays unavailable until a name is entered. Cancel closes the row.

    {{IMG:02-add-district.png}}

    Figure 2. Adding a district inside Morobe Province.

  3. For a new top-level area, use Add Region (the button is named after your top level) at the top of the list.

Step 3: Edit, deactivate or reactivate an area

  1. Click Edit on an area's row to change its name or code.

  2. Use the Active switch to deactivate an area that is no longer used. Inactive areas are hidden from pickers but stay on existing records. Set Status to Inactive or All to find them again.

Tips and notes

  • A district without LLGs is fine. Organizations and sites can point to whichever level you know.
