At a glance

PurposeRegister a new patient so they can be linked to test orders.
You'll end up withA saved patient record, searchable when creating orders.
Who does thisReception staff. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.
Before you startThe patient's identifiers (National ID), name, gender, and date of birth (or age).
WhereMain menu → Patient → Add/Edit Patient → New Patient.

The New Patient form captures a patient's core identity plus optional contact, emergency, and identification details. It is reached from the Add Or Modify Patient screen (see the Patient Management guide for finding existing patients).

Step 1 — Open the New Patient form

  1. Open Main menu → Patient → Add/Edit Patient and click the New Patient tab.

The New Patient form: patient information with expandable sections for contact, additional info, and ID documents.

Step 2 — Enter patient information

  1. Enter the National ID (required) and, where available, the Unique Health ID number.
  2. Enter Last Name, First Name, and optionally Primary phone and Email.
  3. Set Gender (required) and Date of Birth (required) — or enter Age / Months / Days if the exact date is unknown.
  4. Optionally Add Photo.

Step 3 — Add optional details and save

  1. Expand Emergency Contact Info, Additional Information, and Identification Documents to record more where needed.
  2. Click Save (or Clear to reset the form).

Field reference

FieldNotes
National IDRequired. The patient's national identifier — the record cannot be saved without it.
Unique Health ID numberOptional health identifier.
Last Name / First NameThe patient's name.
Gender / Date of BirthRequired. Enter the date of birth, or Age / Months / Days when it is unknown.
Emergency Contact / Additional Information / Identification DocumentsOptional expandable sections for further detail.

Notes

  • Because National ID is required, Save stays unavailable until it (with Gender and Date of Birth) is provided.
  • To find or edit an existing patient instead, see the Patient Management guide.
