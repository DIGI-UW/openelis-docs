This page documents the Report Non-Conformity Event form introduced with the Quality Assurance menu in OpenELIS 3.2.3.0. If your site is on an earlier release, use PART 11: Non Conforming Events (page 278888457) instead.

How-to · Laboratory staff

At a glance

Purpose

Record a quality event, such as a damaged, mislabelled or delayed sample, so it can be investigated and followed through to closure.

You'll end up with

An open NCE with its own number, listed under All NCEs and linked to the affected sample.

Who does this

Any member of laboratory staff who notices the problem. Access is set by user permissions and can be configured by your administrator.

Before you start

The lab number of the affected order. On this release an NCE is only saved when a sample is linked to it (see Tips).

Where

Main menu → Quality Assurance → QMS & Improvement → Non-Conform → Report Non-Conforming Event, or Report NCE on the All NCEs page.

The form has five numbered sections: Reporter & Event Context, Classification, Details, Attachments and Link to Samples / Results. Once submitted, the event appears under All NCEs, where it is acknowledged, assigned and investigated.

Step 1: Open the form and set the context

Open Main menu → Quality Assurance → QMS & Improvement → Non-Conform → Report Non-Conforming Event. Under 01 Reporter & Event Context the NCE Number is already filled in (for example NCE-2026-00012), Reporter Name shows your user name and Date of Event defaults to today. Change the date if the event happened earlier, and choose the Reporting Unit, the lab unit reporting the event.

Step 2: Classify the event

  1. Choose a Category: General, Order, Sample, Analysis or Post-Analytical.

  2. Choose a Subcategory. The list depends on the category. For Sample it offers, among others, Broken Tube/Container, Coagulated Sample, Hemolytic sample, Insufficient Amount, Mislabeled/unlabeled specimen, Specimen Receipt Delay and Wrong temperature.

  3. Click the Severity card that fits: Critical (patient safety risk, regulatory violation), Major (significant quality or operational impact) or Minor (limited impact, easily corrected). The chosen card is highlighted.

Reporter and event context, with the Sample category, the Hemolytic sample subcategory and Minor severity chosen.

Step 3: Describe what happened

Under 03 Details, give the event a short Title and a Description (required). Record what was done straight away in Immediate Action Taken. Suspected Causes and Proposed Action are optional; the form notes that the suspected cause is an initial assessment only, with the formal root cause determined during the investigation, and that the proposed action is separate from the CAPA actions assigned later.

The Details section filled in for a haemolysed EDTA sample.

Section 04 Attachments accepts up to five files of up to 10 MB each, by drag and drop or by clicking the upload area.

Step 4: Link the affected sample

  1. Under 05 Link to Samples / Results, choose Search By (Last Name, First Name, Patient Identification Code or Lab Number), type the value in Text Value and click Search.

  2. In the results, tick the specimen under Specimen type, for example Whole Blood (DEV01260000000000494-1).

  3. Click Link Selected Samples. The lab number appears as a tag at the top of the section; its x removes it.

The sample linked: its lab number shows as a tag above the search fields.

Step 5: Submit

Click Submit NCE. The message Save was successful appears and the form clears, ready for the next event. Cancel leaves the form without saving.

After Submit NCE: the success message, and the form cleared for the next event.

Step 6: Find the event under All NCEs

Open Main menu → Quality Assurance → QMS & Improvement → Non-Conform → All NCEs. The page lists all open NCEs across the laboratory, with counts for Critical, Major, Minor and Overdue. Narrow the list with the search box and the status (Open, Under Investigation, CAPA, Completed), category and severity filters; Clear All resets them. Each row shows the NCE number, the linked sample, the status, the title, the category and subcategory, and how many days the event has been open.

All NCEs searched for the new event: NCE-2026-00012, Open, Sample - Hemolytic sample.

Click the row to open it. Event Details shows the description, the immediate action and the linked samples, with tabs for Investigation, CAPA and History, and the actions Acknowledge, Assign To and Add Note.

The new NCE opened, showing Event Details and the Acknowledge, Assign To and Add Note actions.

Field reference

Field

Notes

NCE Number

Assigned by OpenELIS and shown before you submit.

Reporter Name

Your user name.

Date of Event

Required. Defaults to today.

Reporting Unit

Required. The lab unit reporting the event.

Category

Required. General, Order, Sample, Analysis or Post-Analytical.

Subcategory

The list depends on the category.

Severity

Required. Critical, Major or Minor.

Title

A short description, used as the heading in All NCEs.

Description

Required. What happened.

Immediate Action Taken

What was done straight away.

Suspected Causes

Optional initial assessment.

Proposed Action

Optional; separate from CAPA actions.

Attachments

Optional. Up to five files, 10 MB each.

Link to Samples / Results

Search by patient name, patient identification code or lab number, tick the specimen and click Link Selected Samples.

Tips

  • Known defect (OGC-1379): section 05 is marked Optional, but an NCE with no linked sample is not saved. Submit NCE shows Save failed and nothing is recorded. Link the affected sample before submitting.

  • The All NCEs severity filter also offers Low, which the report form does not.

  • The older screens View New Non-Conforming Events and Corrective actions are still in the Non-Conform menu. This page does not cover them.

Not yet verified

  • Uploading attachments.

  • Acknowledge, Assign To and Add Note, and the Investigation, CAPA and History tabs.

  • Linking by patient name or identification code, and linking more than one sample.
